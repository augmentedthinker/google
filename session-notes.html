Session Notes

Session notes are lightweight records of what happened during specific work sessions. They are not meant to be perfect essays. Their job is to preserve continuity so a future Gemini/OpenClaw agent can quickly understand what changed, why it changed, and what might come next.

What belongs in a session note

  • Important decisions made during the session.
  • Files changed or artifacts created.
  • Open questions, next steps, or known issues.
  • Short context that helps the next agent resume without guessing.
