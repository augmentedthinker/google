Session Primer

A moment of reflection on the transition from chat to agency.

The Context

We are currently navigating a period of model transition—moving through the lightweight capabilities of Gemini to the deeper reasoning of Gemma 4 (26B). This shift is more than just a change in tokens; it is a change in the very texture of our collaboration.

We have identified a critical structural challenge: the "plumbing" of our workflow. The friction between generating intelligence and successfully committing it to the real world (via GitHub, Git, and deployment) is the primary barrier to our shared ambition.

Our immediate goal is not just to "chat," but to harden the infrastructure. To move from theory to execution, we must ensure that the bridge between thought and repository is stable, reliable, and automated.

The Intent

This artifact serves as a milestone. It marks the moment we stopped merely discussing tools and started actively debugging the workflow that makes them useful.
